## 2. Resulting Behavior

- Managed writes require a private, valid, authority-bound recovery-point record from a verified backup. Missing, malformed, stale, future-dated, unbound, untrustworthy, or unreadable evidence denies writes. Evidence is rechecked at commit; reads and separate-path restore remain available while paused.
- The inclusive 45-minute managed-write cutoff leaves 15 minutes before the one-hour maximum. A fresh verified NAS backup is required before writes resume.
- The monitor warns locally when the newest verified copy exceeds 20 minutes and reports healthy coverage after a new verified backup. No external alert service or separate status-file mechanism was added.
- Existing live file-backed behavior is unchanged; the guard is not enabled in production.
