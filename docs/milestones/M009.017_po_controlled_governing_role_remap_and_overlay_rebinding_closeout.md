  matches a recorded image. Unknown edits, invalid fingerprints, unsafe paths,
  or corrupt journals fail closed.
- The coordinator is domain-specific; ordinary Procedure Apply's
  incompatibility protection and the published 1.3.0/1.4.0 release contracts
  remain unchanged.

## 4. Architecture / Integration Notes

The approved Architect dispatch selected Option A: a narrow durable
remap-specific coordinator rather than either a general cross-repository
transaction engine or a documented unsupported boundary. Existing transaction
primitives, state-artifact locking, procedure rendering/status, registry
validation, and exact-image replacement/recovery are reused where applicable.

The transition is staged, not atomic across filesystem roots or service
restart. Generated entrypoints and lock are written first; registry is last.
The intermediate state may be `INCOMPATIBLE`, and the service's stale-registry
guard prevents mutations until restart. A separate local CLI confirmation is
the bounded operator interface; Repo Control does not authenticate the human
operator identity. No Coder live operation is authorized.

## 8. Known Limitations

- The capability has not been deployed or accepted for live operator use.
- No real managed-repository remap, registry cutover, service restart,
  activation, or recovery was performed.
- Per-file atomic replacements and durable checkpoints do not make the
  registry/filesystem/restart sequence atomic; arbitrary external writers are
  not serialized by Repo Control's state-artifact lock.
- Direct dependency reporting covers Markdown links between the mapped
  governing documents; it is not a general document-reference analyzer.
- Repo Control records reported operator actions but does not authenticate
  the human identity behind the CLI confirmation.
