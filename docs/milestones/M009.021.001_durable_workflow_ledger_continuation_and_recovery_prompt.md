# M009.021.001 — Durable Workflow Ledger Continuation and Recovery (WF-01)

## Objective and source of authority
Complete the WF-01 follow-up from accepted M009.021 without changing the original roadmap: M009.022 remains Resource and Execution Safety, and M009.023 remains Retention and Final Limits Acceptance. M009.021.001 is a separately authorized follow-up within the M009.021 Workflow and History arc, NOT a reopening of M009.021.

Today a milestone's schema-v1 workflow sidecar stores the full event history in one file and refuses reads/writes beyond 2,000 events or 5,000,000 bytes. The system must preserve complete, auditable history yet allow legitimate supported milestones to record further events through bounded, recoverable operations. The aim is to redesign the ledger, not move the arbitrary capacity cliff elsewhere. The accepted M009.021 closeout (especially WF-01 design comparison and Phase A fixtures), M009.020 Appendix A, and current governing context are prior evidence, not proof that a particular v2 design is safe.

This milestone addresses WF-01 ONLY. DISP-01 (500-dispatch/40 MiB ledger), PROC-02 (terminal procedure-transaction pruning discrepancy), and INT-05/API-04/DATA-02/DATA-03 remain independently owned issues. They may be read for dependency assessment but may NOT be modified. In particular, do not change the PROC-02 pruning policy here or treat that outstanding finding as fixed. No other managed repository modifications.
