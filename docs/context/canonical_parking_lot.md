## ARCH-001 — Choose the next architecture direction

**Decision needed · PO-owned architecture choice.** Repeated defect loops, release work and recurring backup/governance cost compete with the benefit of reliable single-user handoffs. The five pillars have sufficient functional evidence for a decision; the forward production gaps below remain. Choose one direction after reading the [product baseline](project_context.md) and [architecture alternatives](project_architecture.md#architecture-decision-baseline).

| Option | What would justify the choice |
|---|---|
| Retain and repair | A bounded plan with a finite proof set can close the scheduled launch and frozen-release blockers while retaining the current store and a workable recovery/rollback path. Daily operator effort is acceptable. |
| Simplify/refactor | A concrete seam can remove an ongoing obligation (selection pointer, repeated catalog work, release artifacts or redundant operator steps) with a smaller trusted surface, demonstrable savings, preserved guarantees and reversible migration. |
| Rebuild | A plausible implementation slice preserves the five pillars, exact approvals/revisions and historical authority status, with a compatible import, rollback and validation plan. The full transition cost is credible relative to ongoing effort saved. Reuse the proven boundaries. |
| Defer | Preserve present evidence and avoid scope expansion until a real operator need or materially lower cost justifies proceeding. Optional features may remain here indefinitely. |

The PO sets an acceptable effort ceiling, minimum release/recovery evidence, and operator-burden target before authorizing new work. A viable option preserves repository isolation and Git ownership, exact prompt/closeout decisions, event order, historical provenance and recoverability. If the bounded case fails, stop and choose again rather than expand the milestone. This entry creates neither a new milestone nor authorization.
