How to interpret the measurements

The deliberate button is the reported Product Owner action; identity is not authenticated. No separate checkbox, Git change, or milestone lifecycle change.

How to read Intelligence Value
  • Freshness: queries serve only a CURRENT graph. A refresh receipt records maintenance, not a structural query or proof of value.
  • At a glance: use counts and response estimates describe recorded requests; rated and unrated counts are shown separately. Rating percentages use only the rated-use denominator.
  • Recent uses: open a row for its generation, source-inspection status, and optional feedback. Ratings are subjective reports; MISLEADING ever remains visible after a rating changes.
  • Comparisons: only matched controlled runs with the same fixed reference tokenizer support reference-token reduction. Positive means fewer Intelligence-first evidence tokens; negative means more. Older incomparable records remain available below.
  • Refresh receipts: show graph maintenance attempts and are not evidence queries, benchmark records, or user feedback.
